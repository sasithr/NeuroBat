Video Recording Guidelines

Better video quality improves landmark detection and makes biomechanics measurements more reliable.

Full Body Visible Keep the player's head, shoulders, hips, knees and feet inside the frame.
Stable Camera Avoid unnecessary camera movement while recording the batting action.
Good Lighting Ensure the player is clearly visible and avoid strong shadows or dark footage.
Clear Camera Angle Record from a consistent front or side perspective where the body landmarks remain visible.
